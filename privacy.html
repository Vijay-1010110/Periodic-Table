Privacy Policy for Periodica 3D

Effective Date: September 2026

Periodica 3D is committed to user privacy. This Privacy Policy details our data practices for the Periodica 3D mobile application.

1. Zero Personal Data Collection

Periodica 3D operates as an offline-first educational scientific tool. We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information, device identifiers, geolocation coordinates, contacts, camera data, or financial information.

2. Local Device Storage

User preferences (e.g. temperature scale choices, display view modes, and quiz high scores) are stored strictly locally on your device via standard Android application storage. This data never leaves your device and can be permanently deleted at any time through Android Settings.

3. Network Communications

All core 3D simulations, orbital calculations, isotope decay chains, and equation balancing operate 100% offline. If a user manually initiates an online compound search via the optional PubChem lookup feature, standard secure HTTPS queries are made directly to official public science APIs.

4. Advertising and Tracking

Periodica 3D contains zero third-party advertising SDKs, zero user tracking scripts, and zero commercial analytics.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Because Periodica 3D collects no personal data whatsoever, it is fully safe for learners of all ages and compliant with COPPA and global educational privacy standards.
